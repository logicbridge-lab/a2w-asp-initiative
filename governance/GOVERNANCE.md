# Governance

A2W/ASP is maintained as an open specification project. Maintainers steward the repository, review changes, publish releases, and protect the distinction between normative and advisory material.

## Decisions

Small fixes and non-normative documentation changes need one maintainer approval. A normative protocol, schema, conformance, security, or versioning change requires a public issue, a minimum seven-day comment period, and two maintainer approvals. Material disagreement is resolved by a recorded maintainer decision with rationale.

## Roles

Contributors propose and review work. Maintainers merge accepted changes and publish releases. The project lead appoints maintainers until a contributor election process is adopted. No role grants authority over a domain owner’s published ASP or a client’s user authorization.

## Decision records

Material decisions are recorded under `governance/adrs/` using the ADR template. The first record establishes that Draft 0.1 prioritizes a small, JSON-based discovery contract over a larger action-execution protocol.

## Conduct

Participants must act in good faith, provide reproducible evidence for technical claims, and avoid publishing private data or unsafe crawl artifacts. Security issues follow the process in `spec/security.md`.
