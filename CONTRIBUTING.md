# Contributing to A2W/ASP

Thank you for contributing. Open an issue before proposing a material schema change so that the community can review the use case and compatibility impact. Pull requests must include a clear description, tests for executable changes, and updates to the relevant documentation.

Do not submit crawl data that violates a domain's robots.txt rules, terms of access, or applicable law. Inferred ASP Drafts must retain evidence references and confidence values. Do not present an inferred Draft as domain-authored.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is licensed under Apache-2.0.
